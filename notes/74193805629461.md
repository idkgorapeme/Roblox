## Notes

### Anticheat present
`ZAC_Report` and `ZAC_Jumpscare` are a client-side anticheat (Zap/ZAC
family). `ZAC_Report` fires when it thinks something is off and
`ZAC_Jumpscare` is the punishment. Anything built here should stay
conservative: no teleport spam across the map, no absurd fire rates on
`HitWall` / `Click`. Worth checking what triggers a report before shipping
a farm loop.

### Player data lives in ReplicaService
Stats are not plain attributes here, they come through
`ReplicaRemoteEvents`. Reading cash/pickaxe/stage state means either
`leaderstats` or hooking the Replica data, not `GetAttribute`.

### Likely feature candidates
- Auto mine: loop `Remotes.Server.Click` and/or `HitWall`, listening to
  `Client.UpdateWallHealth` / `Client.BreakWall` for feedback.
- Auto sell: `SellAllLoot` on a timer, or on `Client.LootAdded`.
- Auto rebirth: `Server.Rebirth`.
- Auto upgrade: `UpgradeSlot`, `UpgradeWalkspeed`.
- Auto pickaxe: `PurchasePickaxe` + `EquipPickaxe`, needs ids from
  `Databases`.
- Stage skipping: `SkipStage` / `TeleportToStage` / `GotoSurface`.
- `GroupReward` one-shot claim button.

Arg shapes for all of these are unknown, a spy capture is needed before
building anything.
