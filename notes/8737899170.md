## Notes

- **`SessionToken` attribute + `AC_*` remotes + `__BLUNDER`**: this game
  has real server-side validation and telemetry. It is a big paid-for
  title, not a copy-paste simulator. Anything built here should prefer the
  game's own automation remotes over movement or rate-spam loops.
- **`FlyBorder` in workspace**: flight outside the border is likely
  detected.
- **Remote names with spaces and colons** need `FindFirstChild("Orbs: Collect")`,
  never dot indexing.
- **leaderstats keys carry emoji**, look them up by iterating children or
  matching on a substring.
- The `AutoFarm_*` / `AutoHatch_*` / `AutoRaid_*` remotes are the obvious
  starting point: they are official features, so firing them cannot look
  like cheating. Arg shapes still need a spy capture.
